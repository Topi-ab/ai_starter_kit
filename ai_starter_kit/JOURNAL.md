# JOURNAL

Session journal, newest first. One `## YYYY-MM-DD` section per day, one bullet per entry starting
with `**HH:MM**`, added with `python3 pm/pm.py journal "text"` (it takes the time stamp).
Record what the task files do not show: what was learned, environment facts, problems and their
fixes. Never rewrite old entries; add a correcting entry instead.
